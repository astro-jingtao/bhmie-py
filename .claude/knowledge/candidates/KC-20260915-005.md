exact equality between two compiled implementations of the same math
(benchmark oracles, vendored-vs-upstream copies, cross-unit
reproducible builds). The diagnostic fingerprint: failures only on
FMA-baseline archs, ~1 ULP magnitude, small fraction of elements.
