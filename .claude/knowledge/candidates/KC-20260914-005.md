## Notes

Implication for future perf work: on gfortran the upstream baseline is
already near-optimal — do not chase the 2.17x there. To speed up Linux,
attack the ~24% CLI-orchestration overhead (batching, output paths) or
the algorithm itself. Single-run timings; treat ratios as magnitudes,
not exact constants. Numbers will also shift with flang/gfortran
versions (flang 23.1.1, gcc 15.3.0 as of this record).
